Expertise

Three disciplines. One delivery system.

Nuclear work succeeds when commercial rules, buying decisions and physical delivery remain aligned. ProCM supports that complete chain.

01

Procurement

What should be bought, from whom, and under which requirements?

Strategy and execution that connect technical specifications, qualified supply, nuclear quality assurance, documentation and schedule.

Typical support
  • Procurement strategy and package planning
  • Tender preparation and bid evaluation
  • Supplier identification and qualification
  • Commercial negotiation and purchase orders
  • Inspection, expediting and documentation follow-up
02

Supply chain

How will the right material reach the right place at the right time?

End-to-end control of supplier readiness, manufacturing progress, material flow, logistics, preservation and traceability.

Typical support
  • Supply-chain mapping and risk identification
  • Manufacturing readiness and progress monitoring
  • Export, customs and logistics coordination
  • Material control, preservation and warehousing
  • Delivery sequencing and lifecycle inventory
03

Contracting

Who carries which responsibility and risk?

Clear commercial frameworks for scope, interfaces, quality, schedule, performance, change and long-term obligations.

Typical support
  • Contracting strategy and delivery models
  • Commercial risk and interface allocation
  • Schedule, remedy and warranty structures
  • Change management and claim prevention
  • Supplier and contractor negotiations

Contracting defines the framework. Procurement selects and buys. Supply chain delivers.

Procurement

Specialised sourcing

Supply chain

Legacy continuity

Strategic inventory, life-of-type buys, counterfeit prevention and readiness for critical outages.

Contracting

Outage and modernisation terms

Agreements aligned to plant availability, engineering services and supplier performance.
